# /signup is kept out of search via a per-page noindex meta tag (it must stay
# crawlable for Google to read that tag), not a Disallow rule.
# /admin is the private proposal builder — Disallow-ed (it's client-only, never
# prerendered, and carries a noindex meta tag too).
#
# /quote-* and /approve* are individual customers' quote links. Unlike /signup
# these must not be crawled AT ALL, not merely kept out of the index: each URL
# is one customer's name, address, phone and pricing, and the page also accepts
# a signature. They already carry noindex, and Disallow is the belt to that
# braces — safe here precisely because there is nothing to de-index (these URLs
# have never been public, and a crawler that never fetches one can't leak it).
